Think You Know Yorùbá? Fastest brain wins five!

A quick-thinking team game celebrating Yoruba language, culture and everyday knowledge.

Set up your game

One shared device · 30 seconds per turn

How to play

  1. Divide into teams and take turns.
  2. Roll the die. Its colour selects the question.
  3. For WILD, choose any one of the five questions.
  4. Name five answers before the 30-second timer runs out.
  5. Award one point for each valid answer. Maximum: five points per turn.
  6. Play until every card has been used. The team with the highest score wins!

Players judge their own answers. The game does not check answers automatically.
